Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Overview

Cynor Archive is a personal backup configuration operated by its owner. It is not a public service and does not collect information from other users.

Data Access

The application uses Google Drive API through rclone to store and retrieve the owner's personal backup files. Access is authorized by the Google account owner.

Encryption and Storage

Files and filenames are encrypted locally using rclone crypt before being uploaded to Google Drive. The encryption passwords are controlled by the owner.

Data Sharing

The application does not sell, share, or disclose personal data to third parties. Backup data is stored in the owner's Google Drive account.

Data Collection

The application does not operate analytics, advertising, or independent user tracking services.
